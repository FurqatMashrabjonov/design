About this stay

A cedar-and-glass cabin set on the last ridge before the water. Floor-to-ceiling windows face west, so the whole living room fills with light from noon until sunset. The terrace runs the length of the house with a fire bowl at the north end and a path down to a private cove in about six minutes.
