# Lumen

> Category: Mobile
> iOS 26 on a phone: an opaque, calm content layer under a floating glass control layer, cool light neutrals, concentric corners, one typeface carrying everything by weight and tracking. The platform-native system for 2026.

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

Apple's Liquid Glass, as far as a web page can hold it. Glass is not decoration spread over the screen; it belongs to the controls — the tab bar, a sticky header, a sheet, a floating button — while the content beneath stays opaque and quiet. That separation is the whole idea: the material makes hierarchy, it does not make noise. Glass is never stacked on glass.

## 2. Colour

Cool light neutrals with a tinted canvas, because glass needs something behind it to refract. Content surfaces are pure white. One accent, the platform's blue, used on the primary action, the active tab and links. Status colours keep their literal meaning.

## 3. Type

One face at every size, the way the platform does it: Inter, with the hero carried by weight 700 and −0.035em tracking rather than a second typeface. Tabular numerals for figures.

## 4. Shape & Depth

Radii 12 / 18 / 26px, concentric — a control inside a container takes the container's radius minus its padding. Content cards have a hairline and the faintest lift; the depth lives in the glass above them, blurred 28px with saturation raised so colour bleeds through.

## 5. Layout

Content scrolls under the floating bar rather than stopping short of it. A hero block, then cards and rows on the opaque layer. One filled primary button per screen; the rest of the controls are glass or plain.
