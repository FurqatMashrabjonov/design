# Graphite

> Category: Mobile
> An instrument, not a poster: stepped dark neutrals, hairlines where other systems put shadow, a dense scale, figures in a real mono face and one amber signal used like a dial marking.

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

The register a dark system actually needs. Most dark themes are a light theme with the colours flipped, which is why they read flat — a drop shadow is invisible on a dark canvas, so depth has to come from somewhere else. Here it comes from three stepped surfaces and a one-pixel light edge. Density is the point: this system fits more on a screen on purpose, and trusts the reader.

## 2. Colour

Three neutral surfaces, each a measured step up from the last, with a faint cool cast. One amber accent, bright enough to read as a signal on the dark canvas, used on the primary action and on the one figure that matters — never as a wash. Status colours keep their literal meaning and stay bright.

## 3. Type

IBM Plex Sans for everything, IBM Plex Mono for figures — prices, times, counts, IDs and deltas. The mono is not decoration: it is what makes a column of numbers readable. Tabular figures everywhere.

## 4. Shape & Depth

Radii 8 / 12 / 16px — machined, not moulded. Cards carry a hairline and an inset light edge, never a drop shadow. The bottom bar is part of the chassis: opaque, edge to edge, not floating.

## 5. Layout

Tight rhythm, 44px rows, more rows per screen. A hero figure, then dense rows or a compact two-up grid. One filled amber button per screen; everything else is an outline or plain text.
