# Whisper Violet Precision — style card
Mood: Precise, engineered, quietly premium. White canvas, deep navy type, one saturated violet accent, and cool blue-tinted elevation.
Colour energy: medium — roughly one tenth of a screen carries accent colour; the rest is white, navy and slate.

## Colour use
- Backgrounds are white; text is deep navy for headings, slate for body, dark slate for labels.
- Accent colour is reserved for primary actions, links, active states and focus rings.
- Dark immersive panels use the deep indigo brand-dark surface with white text.
- Success states use green text on a low-alpha green fill with a green border.
- Ruby and magenta appear only in decorative gradients and tinted surfaces, never on buttons or links.
- Borders are a soft blue-grey hairline; selected borders shift to the light violet.

## Type
- One sans family throughout, with a monospace companion for code and technical labels.
- Headlines run at weight 300 with tight line-height and negative tracking that relaxes as size drops.
- Body sits at 16px weight 300–400, line-height 1.40; buttons and links use weight 400.
- Captions 12–13px weight 300–400; micro labels 10px weight 300.
- Numeric data uses tabular figures; code uses the monospace at 12px weight 500 with 2.00 line-height.
- No bold in the sans family: contrast comes from size and colour, not weight.

## Shape and depth
- Radius stays between 4px and 8px: 4px for buttons, inputs and badges, 6px for panels, 8px for featured cards.
- No pill shapes, no radius above 8px.
- Elevation is a two-layer shadow: a far blue-tinted layer with negative spread plus a near neutral layer, giving cool atmospheric depth.
- Ambient lift uses a soft low-alpha neutral shadow; modals use a deeper blue shadow.
- Focus is a 2px violet outline.

## Layout and density
- 390px single column, 16px side padding, 8px base spacing unit with 4/6/8/10/12/14/16 steps.
- Section gaps 40px; card inner padding 16–20px.
- Data blocks are packed tight while the chrome around them stays generous.
- All controls are at least 44px tall, including badges and small buttons.
- Long tables scroll horizontally rather than compressing.

## Signature moves
- Every headline at weight 300 with negative tracking — authority through lightness, never bold.
- Headings in deep navy, never pure black, so text reads warm against white.
- Cards lifted by a blue-tinted far shadow plus a neutral near shadow, both with negative spread.
- One violet accent per screen: a filled primary button, a link, or a selected border — not all three at once.
- Dark indigo panels with white type and translucent white card borders for contrast rhythm.

## Avoid
- Weight 600–700 headlines, or any bold sans text.
- Pill radii, radii above 8px, or fully round buttons.
- Neutral grey or black-only shadows.
- Pure black headings.
- Ruby or magenta on interactive elements.
- Positive letter-spacing at display sizes.
- Warm accent colours for actions.
