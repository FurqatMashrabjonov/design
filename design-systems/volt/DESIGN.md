# Volt

> Category: Mobile
> Dark sport: black stepped surfaces, one volt-lime accent used as a fill with black text on it, condensed heavy italic upper-case headlines that make a screen read like a poster, and hero figures set huge in the condensed face.

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

Energy and effort. A black canvas where nothing competes with two things: the volt accent and the numbers. The headlines are set condensed, italic and upper-case — "START RUN", "TODAY'S OUTPUT" — so the screen has the voice of a training poster, and the figures are the biggest thing on it. Loud on purpose, but only in two places at once.

## 2. Colour

Near-black with two stepped surfaces above it. One volt-lime accent, used as a fill — the start card, the primary button, the active tab, one highlighted bar — always with near-black text on it. Status colours stay bright and small. No second accent.

## 3. Type

Barlow Condensed for headlines and hero figures, weight 800, headlines italic and upper-case with tight leading. Barlow for body and labels. Tabular figures.

## 4. Shape & Depth

Radii 10 / 16 / 22px. Cards carry a hairline and an inset light edge; there is no drop shadow on black. The bar is opaque and edge to edge.

## 5. Layout

A condensed italic headline, a hero figure or a volt start card, then a grid of stat tiles, then a chart with one volt bar, then rows. One volt fill per section, never two side by side.
