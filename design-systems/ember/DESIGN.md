# Ember

> Category: Mobile
> Soft tonal: one warm coral and its own tints, used as surfaces rather than as trim — a hero card filled in coral, charts in its lighter shades, a peach panel for encouragement — on neutral ink, in one heavy geometric sans, with big soft corners.

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

Friendly and energetic without being childish. The whole screen is built from one colour family: the accent fills the hero card, its tints draw the bars and the activity grid, a pale peach holds the encouraging note. Everything that is not coral is quiet — white page, grey cards, near-black text — which is what lets that much colour read as warm instead of loud.

## 2. Colour

A single soft coral and its tints; no second hue. Neutral ink with a whisper of warmth. The text on a coral fill is dark, not white: white on this coral is 2.67:1, and the coral is the identity, so the ink gives way.

## 3. Type

Manrope throughout. Figures are ExtraBold (800) with tight tracking and sit large; headings are Bold; everything else Regular or Medium. No serif, no second face.

## 4. Shape & Depth

Radii 16 / 22 / 30px — nothing sharp. Cards have no border and no shadow; they separate by tone (grey on white, peach, coral). Icons sit in white circles on the grey cards.

## 5. Layout

A hero — either a coral card with one big figure, or a figure alone — then a two-up bento of stat tiles, then a chart or grid in tints, then an encouraging line. One filled coral button per screen. The bar floats, inset, with the active tab as a coral-tinted pill.
