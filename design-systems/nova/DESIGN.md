# Nova

> Category: Mobile
> A calm, expensive phone screen: stone-tinted canvas, squircle cards on soft shadow, a serif hero figure against a tight grotesk, one warm accent used sparingly. The 2026 flagship for consumer apps.

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

Quiet luxury for a phone. Depth comes from air and blur rather than lines; corners are a size up from a 2020 app; the one number that matters is drawn at 88px in a serif and everything else steps back from it. Icons are light strokes in tinted squircles. The tab bar floats.

## 2. Colour

Tinted neutrals (a stone canvas, not grey), white surfaces, a single warm accent. Status colours keep their literal meaning. When this system is chosen automatically, the planner's own palette replaces these colours and only the craft below remains.

## 3. Type

Instrument Serif for the hero figure and screen titles; Geist for everything else, tabular numerals. Two faces, obviously different, the serif at most twice per screen.

## 4. Shape & Depth

Radii 14 / 20 / 28px. Cards have no border and a two-layer soft shadow. Sticky bars and sheets are translucent with a 20px blur.

## 5. Layout

Hero block, then a bento of tiles (one wide, two square), then rows. One filled primary button per screen.
