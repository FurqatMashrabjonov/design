# "5 ideas, 3 made it worse" — LinkedIn carousel

Images: slide-1.png → slide-4.png (upload all four as one post; LinkedIn shows them as a carousel)

## LinkedIn post (no link in the post)

I tested 5 ideas this week to make my AI app designer's output look more "premium".

3 of them made it worse.

✕ Longer screens with more sections
✕ A fancy bento layout as the example
✕ The same bento layout as a ready-made block

✓ Real photos of people instead of initials
✓ A real sign-in + paywall flow, like every top app has

I measure every change the same way: 8 test apps, before and after, judged side by side. The bento grid looked great to me. It still lost.

Lesson: people don't want decoration. They want the app to feel real.

What's the one detail that makes an app feel premium to you?
